Modal Verbs – Mixed Approach

Step 1: Observe

Look at these sentences:

  • I can swim very well.
  • You must wear a helmet.
  • We should study more.

Question: What word comes before the main verb? What does it express?

Step 2: Rule

Rule: Modal verbs (can, must, should, may, etc.) go before the base form of the verb (without “to”). They express ability, obligation, advice, or possibility.

Step 3: Practice

Complete the sentences with the correct modal verb:

He drive a car. (ability)
You eat vegetables every day. (advice)
Students respect the rules. (obligation)
